Sales Data Cleaning & Financial Analysis

> Transforming raw sales data into clean, structured, and insightful business records using Microsoft Excel.

## 🚀 Quick Snapshot
* *What is this?* A hands-on data cleaning and financial calculation project using Excel.
* *Tool Used:* Microsoft Excel (Tables, Filters, Custom Formulas, and Formatting).
* *Goal:* Fix raw data, organize records, and automate financial calculations.

## 💡 What I Did (Key Highlights)
* *Data Cleaning & Formatting:* Organized headers, structured rows, and applied clean table formatting to ensure data consistency.
* *Smart Filtering:* Utilized Excel filters to easily segment transactions by *Sale Type* (Direct Sales vs. Online) and *Payment Mode* (Cash vs. Online).
* *Financial Calculations (Formulas):* Built dynamic formulas to instantly compute crucial business metrics:
  * 💰 *Total Sales* = Quantity × Unit Price
  * 📉 *Total Cost* = Quantity × Unit Cost
  * 📈 *Total Profit* = Calculated margin between sales and costs.
